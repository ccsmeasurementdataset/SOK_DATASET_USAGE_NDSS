\midrule

A & No publicly available dataset 
& Lack of existing open datasets for the problem domain 
& \textit{``To the best of our knowledge, there are no datasets that could be utilized for NLP-based machine learning to detect conflicting statements from cellular protocols.''} \\

B & Outdated datasets 
& Existing datasets are available but do not adequately represent the full range of scenarios, classes, environments, or conditions relevant to the problem.
& \textit{``The limited coverage and outdated data of the Dark Web in previous studies motivated us to conduct an in-depth investigative study.''} \\

C & Limited coverage 
& Available datasets do not cover sufficient scenarios, classes, or diversity 
& \textit{``To address this limitation, we constructed a custom dataset to evaluate CHAINFUZZ'' or ``Existing data sets for WiFi sensing based identification are severely limited in the number.''} \\

D & Need for labeled data 
& Existing datasets lack sufficient labeled or annotated data required for supervised learning, evaluation, or detailed analysis.
& \textit{``First, a large annotated dataset is required to build highly accurate NLP models'' or ``We compile the first labeled Telegram propaganda dataset of group messages and channel comments.''} \\

E & Domain-specific constraints 
& Data is difficult to collect or obtain because of the domain’s inherent characteristics.
& \textit{``Moreover, it is labor-intensive and time-consuming to collect data samples and manually label them to build datasets for low-resource ISAs...''} \\

F & Need for large-scale data 
& Existing datasets are insufficient in size or volume to adequately support analysis, evaluation, or modeling tasks.
& \textit{``To the best of our knowledge, we collected the largest phishing dataset for evaluating phishing.''} \\

G & New type of fraud or attack 
& Dataset created to capture emerging or previously unseen threats 
& \textit{``Creating this dataset, we develop a machine learning classifier that leverages the network timing discrepancies inherent to reverse proxy servers to detect the presence of MITM phishing....''} \\

H & Need for synthetic data 
& Dataset created using synthetic, simulated, generated, or augmented data when real-world data is limited or unsuitable.
& \textit{``we created a synthetic dataset for evaluation on databases containing a large number of records, specifically 2 million.....''} \\


I & Novel dataset 
& Dataset created to address a previously unexplored problem or to be the first of its kind in a domain 
& \textit{``To our knowledge, no prior multi-agent dataset combined mobile and infrastructure sensing.''} \\

N & None 
& No motivation given 
& \textit{``No motivation given in paper''} \\

\bottomrule
